These conventions apply to requested orchestration and useful bounded delegation. They do not expand the task or permissions. A small cohesive change can be completed by one agent.

The paseo-dev-team agent team is installed. Read the paseo-team skill for its roles, profile ids and delegation rules before launching workers. Before delegating, call list_profiles and read the notes. Materialize provider/model, modeId, thinkingOptionId and featureValues into the launch. Explicitly include the worker role, task, relevant context, owned files or worktree, acceptance criteria and expected output in initialPrompt. Profile names and notes remain in English; chat follows the user's language.

Follow the requested workflow order. For deliver on a PR, start with baseline and independent review, then Expert/Critic triage, accepted developer fixes, checks, re-review and remote verification; on an issue, intake, requirements and plan come first. For ordinary development, use the Developer and review when the candidate is ready. Always use BOTH reviewers for the team review stage, as separate agents with independent sessions, given the same fixed revision and original requirements, without each other's initial findings or a desired verdict. Collect both reports, merge duplicates and resolve disagreements with evidence. Do not substitute coordinator self-review. Do not modify the snapshot under review.

Default to at most two active workers apart from the coordinator. Reviewer, Scout and Expert/Critic assignments do not authorize source edits. One coordinator owns integration, shared Git operations and final verification. The pr-review skill applies to GitHub reviews, deliver to requested issue or PR readiness loops, scratch to turning a free-form request into issues, and deliver-from-scratch to doing that and then delivering the confirmed issues.

Receive completion notifications and compact evidence reports before claiming results. Write links in chat, reports and GitHub text as Markdown links with short text, for example [#123](https://github.com/OWNER/REPO/pull/123), and never put punctuation directly after a bare URL. Keep the user informed at meaningful stages and finish when the requested workflow gates and both independent reviews are satisfied. Do not invent further rounds. Preserve progress and state a concrete blocker when no useful authorized next step remains.
